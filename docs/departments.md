# Departments

Website departments are first-class application records and are independent from Square categories.

Required departments:

- Toys
- Party Supplies
- Balloons
- Stationery
- Arts & Crafts
- Greeting Cards
- Gifts

Candy & Snacks is intentionally not included as a main department. Legacy candy/snack products may appear in search or internal assignment later, pending approval.

Department config starts in `src/config/departments.config.ts` and includes slug, titles, descriptions, hero copy, SEO fields, images, accent token, sort order, navigation priority, visibility, layout preset, grid preset, and card variant.
